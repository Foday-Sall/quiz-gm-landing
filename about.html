Our story

Every year, thousands of students sit WASSCE with the same tools: shared past papers, incomplete syllabi, and a teacher's spare time that is stretched thin.

Quiz Gm turns real past papers and your own course PDFs into a plan that learns how you answer: weak topics first, sharp ones on maintenance, marked the way examiners mark.

Why "learns you"

Two students never need the same revision. One loses marks on geometry, the other on comprehension. A single shared study timetable helps neither.

So the plan maps your diagnostic against the syllabus, rebuilds every week from what you missed, and ends exactly on exam week.

What we stand for

Marking you can argue with

Every answer comes back with the reason, the rule, and where the marks went. When the AI is unsure it says so, and points you to the official marking scheme instead of guessing.

Fits the way you already study

Your plan, your weak topics, and your marking all work on the free tier, on any phone with a browser. Lessons and drills are built light, so they load fast on everyday mobile data.

Papers from 2014 to 2025

Nine WASSCE subjects, every year, worked solutions included. Sitting a nursing entrance test or a semester quiz instead? Photograph your past paper and Quiz Gm rebuilds it as practice.
