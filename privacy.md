# Privacy Policy

**Quiz Gm** (quiz.gm) — last updated: September 25, 2026

We keep this short and honest. Here is exactly what we collect, why, and what you control.

## 1. Who we are

Quiz Gm is an exam-preparation service for WASSCE, university, and professional exams, operated from The Gambia. Contact: **support@quiz.gm**.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account basics** — name, email or phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash).
- **Your study data** — answers, scores, streaks, diagnostic results, weak-topic map, uploaded PDFs.
- **Usage basics** — pages visited, quiz events, device type, and approximate network region. We use this to fix bugs and improve the product.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on your study sessions.

## 3. Your uploaded papers

PDFs and photos of papers you upload are used to build your quizzes and are stored privately for your account. They are not shared with other students or published. You can delete any uploaded paper at any time, and it is removed from our systems.

## 4. How we use your data

- Build your personalized study plan and track your progress.
- Mark your written answers and show you why answers are right or wrong.
- Improve question quality and fix errors in past papers.
- Send you service messages (account security, plan reminders) — only ones you have opted into.

## 5. Children and students

Quiz Gm is intended for students preparing for exams. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian to create and manage your account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent; if we learn we have, we delete it.

## 6. Data sharing

We share data only with the processors that keep the service running (for example cloud hosting and AI marking providers), under contracts that limit them to working on our instructions. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## 7. Data retention

Keep your account, keep your data. Delete your account and we delete your personal data and uploaded papers within 30 days, except copies the law requires us to keep.

## 8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and object to or restrict processing, by writing to **support@quiz.gm** or using in-product settings. We respond within 30 days.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and hashed passwords. No service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you without undue delay.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date above shows the current version.

## 11. Contact

Questions about privacy: **support@quiz.gm**.
